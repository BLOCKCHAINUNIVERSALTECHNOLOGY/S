# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We pledge to make participation in the S project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards
- Be respectful and constructive.
- No harassment, hate, or personal attacks.
- Assume good faith; disagree on ideas, not people.
- No publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, or contributions that violate this code,
and may ban contributors for serious or repeated violations.

## Scope
This applies within project spaces and when representing the project publicly.

## Report
Contact maintainers via private channel / security contact listed in SECURITY.md.
